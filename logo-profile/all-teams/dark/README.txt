32 NHL logos rendered by SwiftSVGWebImage to transparent 1200x900 PNGs, aspect-fit with original viewBox margins preserved. Lanczos enabled; two concurrent render slots. Asset URLs come from the official NHL standings API and are recorded in teams.json, including season query strings and the API-selected Washington secondary logo. All logos remain the property of their respective owners and are not covered by the library MIT license. This local collection is for testing and review, not a publication or license grant. Variant: dark. Dark URLs are the corresponding verified _dark.svg assets, preserving season query strings.
